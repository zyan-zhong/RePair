This run, and subsequent descendants, must be identified as containing that intervention; it is not evidence of a completely unattended predeclared acceptance trajectory or of an R2 success-rate gain. Both the original automatic decision and the exception remain immutable and linked. The native governor's promotion branch resets its nonpromotion streak for this intervention.

Future decisions remain automatic under V1.9.4's registered one-seed success-then-goal-fraction rule. Goal progress is restricted to TRAIN_SELECT acceptance, memory and harness are OFF, retained-parent caching is disclosed as reuse rather than a fresh independent measurement.
