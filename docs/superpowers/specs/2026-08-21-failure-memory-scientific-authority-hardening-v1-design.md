# Failure Memory Scientific Authority Hardening V1 — Design

## Objective

Prevent compact summaries, test fixtures, unbound identities, incomplete cell populations, post-outcome rules, hidden writeback, and self-declared cell conclusions from upgrading Q1–Q5.

## Authority chain

A Memory-owned live result must bind:

1. a pre-outcome execution manifest;
2. code, protocol, runtime, policy, environment, panel, schedule, cell-manifest, cell-executor and decision-rule identities;
3. the exact registered cell census;
4. complete receipt/result artifacts for every cell;
5. contamination, writeback, role-pack, round-governance and independent-recomputation audits;
6. a deterministic stage decision recomputed from mechanical cell outcomes.

Cell executors may report observations and costs only. They may not report Q1/Q2/Q3 dispositions.

## Scope boundary

Memory V1 may close Memory-owned Q1–Q3 when real authorities support a conclusion. Q4 needs a separately approved Hierarchical Analyzer plus same-state verifier. Q5 needs separately approved training and Memory-OFF/Harness-OFF/Analyzer-support-OFF evaluation.
