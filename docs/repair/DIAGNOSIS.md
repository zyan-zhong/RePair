# Why local repairs did not become terminal policy gains

## Supported findings

The raw policy sees the public goal, current observation, recent executed history,
interface feedback and complete action menu. The research models inspect public
trajectory evidence. They do not have unrestricted hidden simulator state; this
is an information boundary, not evidence that all observations are missing.

1. A valid local correction often leaves the difficult continuation unchanged.
   R3 reaches a fridge and then cools the egg 25 times. Another R3 branch finds a
   CD and repeats placement without acquiring it. R4 uses a lamp then examines
   the same side table 28 times. These are complete logged branches, not missing
   HTTP responses in the audited causal packages.
2. Earlier candidate and executor representations did not align. Some proposals
   described unsupported free-text termination conditions and were deferred by
   PRE. A later public carried-object predicate also failed to recognize the
   environment's `move ... to ...` menu grammar; the registered correction enabled
   the already valid action. Increasing the number of proposals alone would not
   correct either issue.
3. The later programs cover more prerequisites and produce real terminal benefits.
   However, all 20 successful F1 branches terminate within the harness program,
   with zero policy continuation calls. Five seed repetitions of four programs
   are four source skills, not twenty independently learned capabilities.
4. The training handoff remains sparse: four initial-action targets accompany
   four strategy responses, while the successful programs contain sixteen action
   positions. The model is then tested from full task reset across 355 tasks.
   Sparse source coverage and incomplete execution supervision plausibly limit
   transfer, but their causal contributions have not been isolated.
5. Unassisted failures remain dominated by nonexecution. Parent/candidate have
   261/252 consecutive-nonexecution terminations, 1,556/1,612 out-of-menu calls,
   and 91/100 budget-exhausted tasks. There is no measured net success gain.

Neither more rounds, more paired seeds, nor relaxing promotion alone fixes these
handoffs. The present evidence does not justify calling the temporary regression
a necessary stage before a later capability jump.

## Small, discriminating next experiments (not executed by this release)

First reuse the verified successful suffixes and their exact public observations.
Materialize action supervision at each verified continuation state, preserving
Benefit provenance and the existing action parser. Match parent and total training
budget against the current initial-action-only materialization. Before another
full collection, test whether the candidate can reproduce these same continuations
without the harness. If it cannot learn even these sources, investigate loss/view
balance, capacity and optimization before spending on broad new analysis.

Then test full-reset TRAIN_SELECT with the fixed seed. Address observed invalid
action and prerequisite recovery through verified on-policy examples. Preserve
an unmodified comparison when considering a constrained decoder; otherwise a
runtime improvement could be misreported as an improvement in model weights.
Continue to log terminal effect, process effect and efficiency separately; do not
silently admit process-positive neutral rows into the Benefit-only pool.

## Connections to primary research

- [TrajDebug](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06346v1) distinguishes local errors from
  unresolved errors with terminal impact. The useful adaptation is an explicit
  record of executed repair, first progress, first renewed failure and terminal
  consequence, using existing traces rather than another research subsystem.
- [SIRI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02355) starts with basic interaction competence
  and distills useful skill-guided actions into a plain policy. It highlights why
  our sparse initial-action supervision does not reproduce a successful program.
- [CSO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03412) verifies alternatives followed by the
  learner's own continuation. Harness-completed branches should be explicitly
  distinguished from branches demonstrating such learner continuation.
- [SkillPivot](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.29154v1), a recent September 2026
  preprint, motivates retaining useful prefixes and examining recoverable
  deviations. A bounded teacher-from-the-same-public-prefix diagnostic could
  separate observability limits from proposal/execution limits; it is not proof
  of transfer in our setting.
- [Fission-GRPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15625) studies recovery from execution
  errors. Its task setting and training algorithm differ; the relevant hypothesis
  here is exposure to actual student error states, not an immediate framework rewrite.

These comparisons motivate tests. They do not establish that any proposed change
will improve RePair or that results from other environments transfer unchanged.
